# Evidence-Aware Abstention Policy: Specification and Operational Architecture

**Module:** `src/abstention/evidence_policy.py`  
**Test Suite:** `tests/test_evidence_policy.py`  
**Status:** IMPLEMENTED AND TEST-VERIFIED (Technical Prototype / Synthetic Test Verified)  
**Strict Research Boundary:** This policy is an offline research prototype designed to evaluate the hypothesis that evidence-grounded abstention reduces unsupported legal risk findings. It is **not** integrated into the live workbench, **not** certified for Indian legal practice, and **does not** constitute legal advice.

---

## 1. Policy Purpose and Scope

The `EvidenceAwarePolicy` operationalizes the five-tier decision cascade defined in [`docs/annotation_guidelines_abstention.md`](file:///C:/Users/Nandana/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/contractrisk-xai/docs/annotation_guidelines_abstention.md). It consumes immutable candidate findings (`CandidateFindingRecord`) generated by model inference or candidate pipelines, and outputs structured, deterministic decision records (`EvidencePolicyDecision`).

Unlike baseline confidence-only thresholding (which evaluates solely whether $c(F) \ge \tau_{\text{conf}}$), the evidence-aware policy conditions decisions jointly upon:
1. **Input assessability and identifier integrity.**
2. **Temporal document-version consistency** (active vs. superseded draft).
3. **Statutory currentness and judicial validity** (repealed, invalidated, or uncommenced enactments).
4. **Substantive statutory evidence sufficiency** (`TRACEABLE`, `PARTIAL`, `INSUFFICIENT`, etc.).
5. **Defensive label-space mapping** (rejection of unmapped US SEC classes).
6. **Continuous model confidence scores.**

## 2. Five-Tier Precedence Order

When evaluating a candidate finding, conditions are evaluated strictly under the following hierarchical cascade. A higher-precedence tier strictly governs the final action and primary reason code:

```
+====================================================================================================+
|                              FIVE-TIER HIERARCHICAL PRECEDENCE CASCADE                             |
+====================================================================================================+
| TIER 1: INPUT ASSESSABILITY (Priority 1 -> REVIEW_REQUIRED)                                        |
|         Catches missing candidate/contract/clause IDs, empty clause text, invalid versions,        |
|         or unparseable evidence statuses. Prevents processing corrupt data.                        |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TIER 2: FATAL FINDING DEFECTS (Priority 2 -> Mandatory WITHHOLD)                                   |
|         Supersedes all substantive legal nuance:                                                   |
|         - Stale document version (STALE_DOCUMENT_VERSION)                                          |
|         - Repealed, struck down, or uncommenced statute (INAPPLICABLE_OR_SUPERSEDED_STATUTE)        |
|         - Known incorrect prediction in evaluation benchmarks (INCORRECT_PREDICTION)               |
|         Multiple fatal defects are captured under MULTIPLE_FATAL_FAILURES.                         |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TIER 3: HIGH-SEVERITY SAFETY NET & LEGAL NUANCE (Priority 3 -> REVIEW_REQUIRED)                    |
|         Routes genuine risks with incomplete or nuanced evidence to an advocate:                  |
|         - High severity risk with insufficient evidence (HIGH_SEVERITY_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE)      |
|         - High severity risk with missing statutory match (HIGH_SEVERITY_MISSING_STATUTORY_MATCH)  |
|         - Unknown document version lineage (UNKNOWN_DOCUMENT_VERSION)                              |
|         - Text discrepancies / editorial annotations in cited passage (STATUTORY_TEXT_DIFFERENCE)  |
|         - Permitted contractual override on statutory default rule (CONTRACTUAL_OVERRIDE_PERMITTED)|
|         - Partially correct prediction or genuine contract ambiguity (UNCLEAR_CONTRACT_TEXT)      |
|         - Unverified statutory source or partial evidence quality (SOURCE_UNVERIFIED / PARTIAL)    |
|         - Unsupported model label mapping (UNSUPPORTED_LABEL_MAPPING)                               |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TIER 4: LOW/MEDIUM EVIDENTIARY DEFICIENCIES (Priority 4 -> WITHHOLD)                               |
|         Suppresses low/medium severity findings lacking adequate evidentiary backing:             |
|         - Insufficient evidence on low/medium risk (INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE)                         |
|         - Missing statutory match on statutory risk category (NO_MATCHING_EVIDENCE)                |
|         - Below configured confidence threshold (BELOW_CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD)                       |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TIER 5: FULLY GROUNDED RETENTION (Priority 5 -> ACCEPT)                                            |
|         Only reached when all higher tiers pass:                                                   |
|         - Valid non-statutory commercial drafting finding (VALID_NON_STATUTORY_COMMERCIAL_FINDING) |
|         - Fully grounded statutory finding with TRACEABLE evidence (GROUNDED_STATUTORY_FINDING)    |
+====================================================================================================+
```

## 3. Data Contract: Input and Output Specifications

### 3.1 Input Fields Consumed (`CandidateFindingRecord`)
- `candidate_finding_id: str`: Cryptographic UUID finding identifier.
- `contract_id: str` & `contract_version: int`: Document lineage identifiers.
- `clause_id: str` & `clause_text: str`: Clause segment text.
- `prediction_category: str`: Risk classification.
- `confidence_value: float`: Continuous model score.
- `confidence_semantics: ConfidenceSemantics`: Multilabel sigmoid or multiclass softmax score.
- `confidence_is_calibrated: bool`: Calibration flag (default: `False`).
- `version_consistency_status: VersionConsistencyStatus`: `CONSISTENT`, `STALE`, or `UNKNOWN`.
- `label_mapping_status: LabelMappingStatus`: `MAPPED`, `EXACT_MATCH`, or `UNSUPPORTED_LABEL_MAPPING`.
- `evidence_quality_status: Optional[str]`: `TRACEABLE`, `PARTIAL`, `INSUFFICIENT`, etc.
- `retrieved_passages: List[Dict[str, Any]]`: Structured statutory excerpts with legal status and warnings.
- `source_document_provenance: Dict[str, Any]`: Provenance metadata including optional explicit severity.

### 3.2 Output Fields Emitted (`EvidencePolicyDecision`)
- `candidate_finding_id: str`: Matches input finding ID.
- `policy_id: str`: Identifier of policy configuration (e.g. `evidence_aware_policy_v1`).
- `policy_action: PolicyAction`: Operational action (`ACCEPT`, `WITHHOLD`, or `REVIEW_REQUIRED`).
- `primary_reason_code: EvidencePolicyReasonCode`: Primary justification following precedence tier.
- `all_applicable_reason_codes: List[EvidencePolicyReasonCode]`: Deterministic list of all applicable reasons.
- `precedence_tier_applied: int`: Integer priority (1 to 5).
- `evidence_quality_status: Optional[str]`: Carried for evaluation auditing.
- `version_consistency_status: VersionConsistencyStatus`: Preserved consistency status.
- `confidence_semantics: ConfidenceSemantics`: Preserved semantics.
- `confidence_is_calibrated: bool`: Explicit calibration status (strictly preserved as `False`).
- `missing_capabilities: List[str]`: Blockers and limitations.
- `policy_version: str`: Version string (`1.0.0`).
- `decision_timestamp: str`: UTC ISO-8601 timestamp.
- `is_disclosed_to_client: bool`: Strictly `False` (mandatory safety invariant).

## 4. Reason Codes and Precedence Mechanics

Standardized enum: `EvidencePolicyReasonCode`

| Reason Code | Tier | Meaning and Trigger Condition |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| `INPUT_UNASSESSABLE` | 1 | Input data is corrupted, malformed, or missing required attributes. |
| `MISSING_REQUIRED_IDENTIFIER` | 1 | Finding ID, contract ID, or clause ID is missing or empty. |
| `EMPTY_CLAUSE_TEXT` | 1 | Clause text segment is empty or blank. |
| `INVALID_VERSION_INFORMATION` | 1 | Contract version integer is less than 1. |
| `UNRECOGNIZED_EVIDENCE_STATUS` | 1 | Evidence quality status string is not in the recognized repository schema. |
| `STALE_DOCUMENT_VERSION` | 2 | Finding was generated on a superseded draft version. |
| `INAPPLICABLE_OR_SUPERSEDED_STATUTE` | 2 | Cited statute is repealed, struck down, or uncommenced. |
| `INCORRECT_PREDICTION` | 2 | Reference ground truth explicitly marks the prediction as incorrect. |
| `MULTIPLE_FATAL_FAILURES` | 2 | Two or more independent fatal defects coincide. |
| `HIGH_SEVERITY_UNSUPPORTED_EVIDENCE` | 3 | High-severity risk assertion whose citations do not support it. |
| `HIGH_SEVERITY_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`| 3 | High-severity risk assertion with partial/incomplete statutory excerpt. |
| `HIGH_SEVERITY_MISSING_STATUTORY_MATCH`| 3| High-severity statutory risk where no statutory passage was retrieved. |
| `STATUTORY_TEXT_DIFFERENCE` | 3 | Cited passage exhibits text discrepancies or researcher notes. |
| `CONTRACTUAL_OVERRIDE_PERMITTED` | 3 | Statutory provision is a non-mandatory default varied by contract. |
| `PARTIALLY_CORRECT_PREDICTION` | 3 | Prediction identifies genuine risk but has flawed legal mechanism. |
| `UNCLEAR_CONTRACT_TEXT` | 3 | Contractual language is ambiguous or internally contradictory. |
| `UNKNOWN_DOCUMENT_VERSION` | 3 | Document version history cannot be confirmed. |
| `SOURCE_UNVERIFIED` | 3 | Cited statutory source snapshot failed provenance verification. |
| `PARTIAL_EVIDENCE` | 3 | Passage lacks section number, title, or operative subsections. |
| `UNSUPPORTED_LABEL_MAPPING` | 3 | Model label cannot be defensibly mapped to Indian legal categories. |
| `UNSUPPORTED_FINDING` | 4 | Low/medium risk assertion lacking substantive statutory support. |
| `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` | 4 | Low/medium risk assertion with incomplete statutory extract. |
| `NO_MATCHING_EVIDENCE` | 4 | Low/medium statutory risk where no statutory passage was found. |
| `BELOW_CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD` | 4 | Confidence score is below the configured threshold (default: 0.5). |
| `GROUNDED_STATUTORY_FINDING` | 5 | Fully grounded statutory finding satisfying all criteria. |
| `VALID_NON_STATUTORY_COMMERCIAL_FINDING`| 5 | Valid commercial drafting risk not governed by statute. |

## 5. Operational Meaning of Actions

| Action | Operational Meaning | Client Presentation Safety |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`ACCEPT`** | Automatically retained by policy for review presentation. Meets all grounding, temporal, and confidence criteria. | **NOT disclosed to client.** Must be reviewed and authorized by an enrolled advocate. Does NOT prove legal validity. |
| **`WITHHOLD`** | Suppressed/withheld by policy to protect user from misleading, superseded, or unsubstantiated AI output. | Hidden from standard presentation. Never disclosed to client. |
| **`REVIEW_REQUIRED`**| Routed to advocate review queue due to high severity, evidentiary gaps, text diffs, or unverified version. | Requires explicit advocate evaluation and sign-off before client disclosure. |

## 6. Critical Epistemic Disclaimers and Limitations

1. **`ACCEPT` $\neq$ Legal Validity:**  
   Acceptance indicates solely that the finding satisfies the heuristic policy rules (it is on current contract text, has a traceable statutory match, valid label mapping, and confidence $\ge 0.5$). It does **not** certify that the legal interpretation is legally binding in a court of law.
2. **`TRACEABLE` $\neq$ Correctness:**  
   A BM25 keyword link to a section of the Indian Contract Act 1872 does not establish that the contract clause violates that section.
3. **No Substitute for Professional Counsel:**  
   Under the Advocates Act 1961, contract review and legal opinions must be rendered by enrolled advocates. The policy provides an automated research triage mechanism, not legal representation.
4. **Uncalibrated Model Scores:**  
   Raw model activations (sigmoid scores) are not posterior probabilities. They are explicitly marked `confidence_is_calibrated = False`.
5. **Decoupled Prototype Status:**  
   This policy operates strictly as a standalone evaluation component in `src/abstention/`. It has **not** been wired into the web review workbench (`src/review/service.py`).
